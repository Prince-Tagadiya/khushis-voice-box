The box automatically goes into battery-saving sleep after 45 seconds of quiet. Simply press any fruit button to wake it instantly with zero lag.

How to Connect & Setup via Web Studio

No apps to download from app stores. The Web Studio runs directly in your browser on Chrome, Safari, or Edge.

  1. Open the Link: Visit https://prince-tagadiya.github.io/khushis-voice-box/ on your phone, tablet, or laptop.
  2. Tap "Connect Voice Box": Select your VoiceBox from the Bluetooth list. It pairs instantly over secure Bluetooth.
  3. Setup Wizard (First Time):
    • Personalize your device name (e.g. Khushi's Voice Box).
    • Set your private 4-digit Security PIN.
    • Choose your preferred starting voice (Male or Female) and test the speaker.
  4. Unlock Anytime: On future visits, simply tap Connect, enter your 4-digit PIN, and your private dashboard opens.

Helpful Tips for Caregivers

  • Emergency Restart: If the box ever needs a fresh reboot, simply press and hold the Banana button for 3 seconds. It will chime and restart smoothly.
  • 1-Click Wireless Updates: Whenever a new software improvement is available, an "Install Update" banner appears. Tap it to upgrade your box wirelessly over Bluetooth in 60 seconds.
  • Low Battery Care: Charge using standard 5V USB. A gentle confirmation chime sounds when powered on.
